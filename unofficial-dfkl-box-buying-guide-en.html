Buying from overseas

Stores that ship directly

  • CDJapan: ships worldwide. Shipping can be calculated before ordering
  • HMV&BOOKS online: ships by EMS, from ¥1,550. Customs duties are paid by the recipient

Stores via a forwarding / proxy service

  • Rakuten Books: can be sent overseas via Rakuten Global Express
  • Tower Records Online: Buyee / WorldShopping / Tenso.com can be officially used

Other stores may also be available through proxy services such as ZenMarket.

Stores not available from overseas

  • Joshin: Orders addressed to forwarding services and purchases made through proxy services are officially subject to cancellation.
  • Seven Net Shopping: no international shipping, and overseas-issued cards are not accepted

Amazon.co.jp

Amazon.co.jp international shipping (AmazonGlobal) is available for eligible items only. Whether this item is eligible is unconfirmed.

Playback: Japanese Blu-ray discs are Region A; DVDs are Region 2 / NTSC. Please check your player's region and format.

Coupon & points notes

ABC Mikke

Add the Rakuraku Mart official LINE account as a friend and register as an ABC Mikke member to get a ¥500 OFF coupon (for orders of ¥2,500 or more including tax, one-time use only, cannot be combined with other coupons).

ABC Mikke top page

Amazon.co.jp

Besides 1% points, linking a d Account gives the regular 1% in d Points (for orders of ¥5,000 or more including tax). For first-time linking, the first purchase made within the same month as the entry and linking earns 10% back in d Points (capped at 500 points).

The 4,000 points for new Amazon Mastercard sign-ups are not included in this price comparison, as they require signing up.

Amazon.co.jp top page

Yodobashi.com

Gold Points 10% back (Blu-ray: 2,146 points / DVD: 1,767 points). Use this as a guide when comparing after deducting the points from the listed price.
